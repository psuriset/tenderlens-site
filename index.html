What it detects

Signals that matter in procurement review.

Vendor concentration

One vendor repeatedly wins the same department at unusually high rates and values.

Repeated directors

Multiple vendors share directors or related ownership patterns that suggest linked entities.

Single-bid tenders

Procurement events with low competition, weak participation, or suspiciously narrow bidder activity.

Fast-turn awards

Contracts awarded unusually quickly after tender close or with repeated patterns in the same office.

Graph intelligence

Turn contracts into relationship maps.

TenderLens connects vendors, directors, companies, departments, and awarded contracts into one explainable graph so teams can see linked patterns quickly.

What it detects

Signals that matter in procurement review.

Vendor concentration

Example: a single infrastructure vendor wins 47 contracts from the same department worth ₹1,200 Cr with an 89% win rate.

Repeated directors

Vendor A and Vendor B share directors, addresses, or linked entities. Potential shell company behavior becomes visible.

Single-bid tenders

Highlight procurement events with low competition, late participation, or last-minute bid behavior.

Timing anomalies

Detect awards made unusually fast after tender close, repeated patterns in the same office, or contract amendments that spike value later.

FAQ

Questions early buyers will ask.

Does TenderLens claim corruption automatically?

No. It ranks anomalies, linked patterns, and evidence trails so humans can investigate with context.

What makes it different from simple OCR or contract search?

TenderLens combines extraction, ownership resolution, graph intelligence, anomaly scoring, and explainable outputs.

Can it work with uploaded PDFs as well as portal data?

Yes. The platform is designed for both portal ingestion and uploaded contracts, notices, annexures, and disclosures.

Why start in India?

India has large public procurement volume, fragmented state and ministry portals, and a strong need for unified transparency tooling.
